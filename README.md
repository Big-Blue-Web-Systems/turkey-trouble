# Turkey Trouble

Thanksgiving-themed luck game — press your luck, or bank before you hit a turkey.

## Play

Camera access needs localhost:

```bash
npx serve .
```

Open the URL it prints, allow the camera, then **Add Players**.

## Rules

- Add players and drag to set turn order, then Start Game
- One action button: **Pull** → **Fire** → **Pull** again
- After a successful fire, **Bag Cash** ends your turn and saves your score
- Landed prizes become Turkeys; hit a Turkey and your turn score is wiped
- Reaction photos snap ~0.75s after Fire
